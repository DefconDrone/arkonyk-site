The challenge

Unrecognized transactions are an issuer problem

Visa estimates that more than half of all disputes begin with a transaction the cardholder simply doesn't recognize. For issuers, that confusion lands directly on your P&L.

Why now

The networks are mandating clarity

Enriched merchant data is becoming a requirement, not a nice-to-have. Mastercard already requires issuers across the Europe region to display enhanced merchant data — the merchant's real name, logo, location, and contact details — in banking apps and digital channels, with non-performance assessments for banks that don't. Visa and Mastercard alike are steering statements toward enriched merchant data and digital receipts.

  • The exact fields required — name, logo, location, contact — are what Descriptors.com aggregates
  • Mandates create the obligation to display — Descriptors.com supplies the data to meet it
  • Getting ahead of the mandate turns a compliance cost into a cardholder-experience win
From Mastercard's enhanced merchant data standard

“Friendly fraud is often the result of unrecognizable merchant identifiers in transaction details. Cardholders mistakenly initiate chargebacks because they do not recognize the purchase or merchant descriptor. Many chargebacks may be avoided by providing cardholders with a more detailed description of the transaction.”

Mastercard AN 4569 — Standards for the Display of Enhanced Merchant Data by Issuers
