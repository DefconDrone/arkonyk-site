Software that brings the truth forward

Three focused products — Descriptors.com, FeeSuite, and ARKITEKT — that give you visibility, control, and strategy across how you accept payments. Use them independently or together.

A payment descriptor management platform. The descriptor is the merchant name a cardholder sees on their statement — and one of the most overlooked causes of disputes. Visa estimates 50%+ of cardholder disputes begin because a customer doesn't recognize a transaction.

  • Register, manage & validate descriptor portfolios
  • Associate logos & business info; identify conflicts
  • Improve recognition for merchants, acquirers & issuers

A B2B SaaS platform that automatically reconciles your acquirer and processor statements every month and keeps a complete history of them. FeeSuite calculates your true cost of payments and surfaces anomalies, errors, duplicate charges, excessive fees, and other opportunities to manage and lower what you pay.

  • A complete, searchable history of every statement
