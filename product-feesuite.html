The problem

Your true cost of payments is buried in statements

Interchange, scheme fees, processor markups, and assessments arrive across fragmented statements in formats built to obscure rather than explain. Reconciling them by hand is slow, error-prone, and rarely done — so overcharges, duplicate fees, and billing errors go unnoticed month after month.

  • Fragmented statements across acquirers, processors, and networks
  • Scheme and network fees too opaque to reconcile with confidence
  • Manual reconciliation that eats finance-team hours every month
  • Overcharges and duplicate fees that quietly erode margin
FeeSuite
Every statement, normalized into one clear cost picture.
What it does

Reconciliation made easy

Automated monthly reconciliation

FeeSuite reconciles your acquirer and processor statements every month — automatically, without spreadsheets or manual matching.

A complete statement history

Every statement is kept in one searchable place, giving you a durable, auditable record of what you were charged and when.

Your true cost of payments

See your real effective rate calculated automatically — the all-in cost of acceptance, not the headline number on a rate sheet.

Anomalies & errors surfaced

Duplicate charges, excessive fees, and unexpected changes are flagged for you — so you can question them and recover margin.
