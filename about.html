About Arkonyk

Truth in Payments.

Arkonyk is a payments intelligence company — solving longstanding problems in new ways for the banks, merchants, and customers who move money every day.

Mission

Arkonyk exists to bring truth to payments. We help merchants understand what the payments industry too often makes difficult to see—what their contracts really mean, what payments truly cost, why disputes happen, and where value is being lost. We turn complexity into clarity, then build and implement practical solutions that create better outcomes. Working across the payments ecosystem, we make payments more transparent, understandable, and accountable for everyone who serves the merchant.

Approach

The payments industry has long agreed on the problems: charges customers can't recognize, disputes that shouldn't happen, costs no one can fully account for, data scattered across parties who don't share it. For decades these problems have been managed, tolerated, and even monetized — but not solved.

Arkonyk exists to solve them. We start from a single principle — truth — and apply new thinking to old problems, building neutral infrastructure that brings the key players in payments onto common ground. The problems the industry shares are the ones it should solve together.
