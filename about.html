About Arkonyk

Truth in Payments.

Arkonyk is a payments intelligence company — solving longstanding problems in new ways for the banks, merchants, and customers who move money every day.

Our mission

Arkonyk — our name merges two guiding principles: to lead, and to aspire. Our customers are Acquiring Banks, Issuing Banks, and the Merchants and Cardholders they serve. We are focused on solving longstanding problems in new ways, leveraging our experience, relationships, and innovative technology.

Our story

Clarity across the payment lifecycle.

Payment descriptors, processing fees, and acceptance strategy are among the most overlooked — and most expensive — parts of the payments ecosystem. Confusing statements drive disputes; fragmented fees hide overcharges; complex stacks grow without a blueprint.

Arkonyk brings these together. Descriptors.com reduces confusion and disputes, FeeSuite reveals and reduces cost, and ARKITEKT — our advisory practice — designs the strategy. Each solves a real problem on its own; together they cover the complete payment lifecycle.

Leadership

Built by operators, not spectators

Arkonyk is led by two people who have spent their careers on opposite sides of the payments table — and got tired of watching the same problems go unsolved.

RL

Rick Lynch

Founder & CEO

Rick's career spans disputes, chargebacks, fraud, and the acquiring side of payments. He was SVP of Business Development at Verifi, acquired by Visa, and Chief Strategy Officer at ChargebackHelp (CBH), acquired by Global Payments — two landmark exits in payments risk — and went on to lead fraud, risk, and disputes at Global Payments. Having sat on the network, acquirer, and solution-provider sides of the payments table, he brings an end-to-end view of where the ecosystem breaks. As Founder & CEO of Arkonyk, Rick leads the company and its core infrastructure, including Descriptors.com — putting acquirers, ISOs, and merchants back in control of transaction identity and financial truth.

JH

Jordan Harris

CMO & Head of Payment Strategy

Jordan brings fifteen years on the merchant side of payments, leading fraud, risk, and chargeback operations at global enterprises including iHerb, Ticketmaster, and Fandango/NBCUniversal. At iHerb he helped protect a business shipping to 185+ countries and stood up two new departments in chargebacks and abuse prevention. He's also the host and producer of Fraud Boxer, an independent podcast where payments and fraud practitioners share how the work actually gets done. At Arkonyk, Jordan leads payment strategy and brand — turning hard-won merchant experience into tools the whole ecosystem can use.
