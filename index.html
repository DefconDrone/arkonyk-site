Why Arkonyk

Neutral infrastructure, built by operators.

Arkonyk exists to put truth back into payments — clear transaction identity, true cost, data you can trust. It's shared infrastructure built by people who've run this business, and it answers to no network, processor, or acquirer.

Built by senior operators

Arkonyk comes from people who've run acquiring, disputes, and risk — and built and sold payments companies before. Experience applied to your hardest decisions, not a generic dashboard.

Independent by design

Truth needs a neutral source. We're not an acquirer, processor, or network — and our charter keeps it that way — so the data and guidance you get serve you, not a vendor's agenda.

One source of truth

An authoritative, acquirer-sourced record connecting issuers, acquirers, merchants, and cardholders — a shared data layer instead of fragmented, siloed tools.

Upstream, not after the fact

Most tools manage cost and disputes after the damage. Arkonyk works at the source, surfacing recognizable charges and true cost so fewer problems start — with no APIs or integration required.
